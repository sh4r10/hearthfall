# Hearthfall: story and core idea

## The world

The sun didn't go out. It dimmed. Over a few years its output fell to a fraction of what it had been. Its gravity never changed, so the Earth kept its orbit, but too little light and heat reached the ground. Crops failed, then the oceans began to freeze. People called the day the last harvest failed **the Hearthfall**.

The survivors went underground, towards the planet's own heat, and they carried fire with them. The sun still rises as a pale disc, so days on the surface are bitter rather than lethal, and nights are lethal everywhere the fire doesn't reach.

## What the player does

You lead a small group of survivors who have dug into the rock. Their hearth is everything: it is light, warmth and safety.

- **Light is territory.** A hearth lights the cave around it. Light only travels through open space, so walls cast shadows. Colonists won't walk into the dark on their own, and they grow cold and die if they stay there.
- **Fire costs something.** Hearths burn fuel (coal, dug from veins in the rock) and eat oxygen while giving off carbon dioxide and heat. A sealed room with a big fire suffocates, and a hearth without oxygen goes out.
- **Nights shrink the light.** Every day ends in a long cold night where hearth light reaches less far. You need enough fuel and enough hearths to get through it.
- **Deeper is warmer, and stranger.** (Planned.) The rock warms as you dig down, but older things live in the deep dark.

## Modes (planned)

- **Solo:** survive as many days as you can. Your score goes on the leaderboard.
- **Co-op:** friends share one colony and one set of hearths.
- **Competitive:** players dig into the same world from different sides. Physics is a weapon: breach a wall to flood or gas a rival's base, or race them to the same coal seam.

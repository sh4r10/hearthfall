# Art prompts: batch 1

The first batch of AI image prompts for Hearthfall. The in-game world is now low-poly 3D built in code (see [art-brief.md](art-brief.md)), so these are kept for the story intro, UI icons, marketing art and sprite experiments.

## How to use these

1. Use any good image tool (ChatGPT image generation, Midjourney, or similar). Start every prompt with the **style block** below, word for word.
2. Generate **#1 (the character sheet) first**. Once you have one you like, attach it as an image reference to every later prompt so the style stays consistent.
3. Generate a few options for each and keep the best. Square images are fine unless the prompt says otherwise.
4. Upload the keepers to the project thread, named by their number (for example `03-coal.png`). I'll cut them out, clean them up and put them in the game.

## Style block (start every prompt with this)

> 2D hand-painted video game asset, strictly side view, in the style of Oxygen Not Included mixed with Don't Starve. Warm dark-brown ink outlines with slightly uneven line weight, muted desaturated colours, soft painted shading lit from the upper left, subtle brush texture. Cosy but cold underground survival mood.

## Prompts

**#1 Survivor character sheet.** (style block) Character turnaround sheet of one survivor, about 2.5 heads tall with a big head, short body and stubby limbs, wearing a hooded winter coat with fur trim, a red wool scarf, mittens, patched trousers and heavy boots. Front, side and back views, plus three facial expressions (content, cold and shivering, worried). Plain light grey background, evenly spaced, nothing overlapping.

**#2 Survivor body parts.** (style block, with #1 attached as reference) The same character from #1 split into separate body parts for puppet animation, laid out with space between them: head, hood front, hood back, open eyes, closed eyes, three mouth shapes, torso with coat, upper arm, lower arm with mitten, upper leg, lower leg with boot. Side view only. Plain light grey background.

**#3 Rock texture.** (style block) Seamless tileable texture of dark cave rock, cool blue-grey stone with painted cracks and lumps, no outlines, flat even lighting, fills the whole square, no edges or border.

**#4 Dirt texture.** (style block) Seamless tileable texture of packed underground soil, warm browns with small pebbles, roots and faint horizontal layers, no outlines, flat even lighting, fills the whole square.

**#5 Coal texture.** (style block) Seamless tileable texture of a coal seam, near-black chunky facets with a few warm golden glints, no outlines, flat even lighting, fills the whole square.

**#6 Cave back wall.** (style block) Seamless tileable texture of a distant cave back wall, very dark warm brown rock with soft layered strata, low contrast and slightly blurred so it reads as background, no outlines.

**#7 Hearth.** (style block) A small campfire hearth for an underground colony: a ring of rough grey stones with crossed charred logs, no flame (the flame is separate), side view, sitting on the ground. Isolated on a plain light grey background, wider than tall.

**#8 Flame animation.** (style block) Sprite sheet of a single campfire flame in 8 frames in a row, looping flicker animation, bright yellow-white core fading to orange and red edges, no logs or stones. Plain black background.

**#9 Ladder.** (style block) A rough wooden ladder made of scavenged planks lashed with rope, straight-on side view, exactly one rung section that can repeat vertically, isolated on a plain light grey background.

**#10 Mushroom bed.** (style block) A low wooden planter box of dark soil with pale glowing lavender cave mushrooms of different heights, side view, wider than tall. Isolated on a plain light grey background.

**#11 Oxygen diffuser.** (style block) A small scrap-built machine that releases oxygen: a teal metal canister with riveted panels, a little pressure gauge, a vent on top with small air bubbles rising. Side view, taller than wide. Isolated on a plain light grey background.

**#12 Story intro, scene 1.** (style block, but change "video game asset" to "wide painted illustration") A small town at midday, but the sun is pale and weak, the light cold and blue-tinged. People in coats glance up at the sky uneasily. 16:9, cinematic.

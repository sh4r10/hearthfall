Hearthfall

Lead a handful of survivors into the warm dark under the ice. Dig for coal, keep the hearth burning, and get everyone through the night.

  • You give orders, survivors carry them out. Pick a tool, then click the rock. Shift-drag to mark many cells.
  • Coal is fuel. Dig the black seams in the walls of your cave. Your firekeeper feeds the hearth.
  • Light is safety. Out of the light, survivors freeze. Nights shrink the light.
  • Mushrooms grow out of the firelight, so build beyond the fire, and light the farm with lamps so Shades keep away.
  • Drag to look around, scroll to zoom.
